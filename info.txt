[STEAM_TITLE:First Person View]
[STEAM_DESCRIPTION:Adventure mode in first person.

Press Ctrl-Shift-F or the camera button and the map turns into a 3D world built from the game's own art. Several levels are drawn at once, the land goes on to the horizon under a real sky, and the rest of the game keeps working around it. Press it again to get the normal map back.

Requires DFHack, on Windows. The mod installs its DFHack plugin by itself the first time the game starts. Nothing is changed in your world or your saves.]
